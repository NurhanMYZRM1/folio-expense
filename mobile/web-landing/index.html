Folio

A local-first expense workspace. Import receipts, review what was read, and export claim reports. Your data stays on your device.

Reads receipts

Photos, PDFs and iPhone HEIC images are read on-device. Online AI is optional.

Works offline

No account and no cloud sync required. Receipts and claims live on your device.

Claim reports

Group expenses into a claim and export a PDF with receipt references, plus CSV or Excel.

Desktop and iPhone

A desktop app and an iPhone app share the same receipt-reading and claims core.

Preview

Folio runs as a desktop app and as an iPhone app (currently an internal development build). There is no web version of the app itself: it keeps its data on your device, so it can't run in a browser yet.
