## Blockers that need the owner

1. Google and Apple are **disabled** in the hosted project (`/auth/v1/settings` reports `google: false`, `apple: false`). See `docs/ACCOUNTS.md` for the exact console steps. Until then browser sign-in returns Supabase's "provider is not enabled" error; email sign-in keeps working.
2. Adding a provider to the existing owner also needs **manual linking** enabled in the project's Auth settings.
3. The physical iPhone was unavailable to Xcode during this work.
